## Shared conventions (all units follow; report a conflict instead of diverging)

- **Plan**: `<target>/<Name>/hpo/plan.json`, schema 1:
  `{"schema": 1, "objective": {"metric": "<name>", "direction": "minimize"|"maximize"}, "sampler": {"name": "TPESampler", "seed": <int>}, "pruner": {"name": "MedianPruner"|"none", "nStartupTrials": <int>, "nWarmupSteps": <int>}, "nTrials": <int>, "maxEpochs": <int>, "nested": {"outerFolds": <int>, "innerValidation": "<text>"}, "arms": ["<arm id>", ...], "space": [{"name": "<param>", "kind": "float"|"int"|"categorical", "low": <num>, "high": <num>, "log": <bool>, "choices": [...]}], "fixed": ["<independent variable the experiments test>", ...], "importance": {"evaluator": "fanova"|"ped-anova"|"mean-decrease-impurity", "seed": <int>, "check": "<second evaluator or null>"}}`.
  Rules `plan` enforces (refusal codes in the skill): one `nTrials` for every arm (equal budget, baselines included); sampler seed required; at least one space parameter, valid bounds, `log` needs `low > 0`, categorical needs ≥2 choices; a parameter in both `space` and `fixed` is refused (an independent variable under test is never tuned); `outerFolds > 1` requires the inner validation statement (search inside each outer fold's training subjects only; test subjects never seen); importance evaluator from the closed set, a seed required for `fanova` and `mean-decrease-impurity`. Optuna 5.0's default importance evaluator is PED-ANOVA, so the plan always names its evaluator.
- **Study naming**: one Optuna study per arm and outer fold, `study_name = "<arm>-fold<k>"`, in one RDB storage (`sqlite:///<target>/<Name>/hpo/studies.db`).
- **Report** (written by `report`, under the target venv that has optuna and matplotlib): per study `<target>/<Name>/Results/hpo/<study>/summary.json` plus figures `contour.png|pdf` (matrix of the top three parameters by importance, or every pair when fewer), `importance.png|pdf`, `history.png|pdf`:
  `{"schema": 1, "study": "<arm>-fold<k>", "arm": "<arm>", "fold": <int|null>, "objective": {"metric", "direction"}, "sampler": {"name", "seed"}, "pruner": "<name|none>", "nTrials": <int>, "complete": <int>, "pruned": <int>, "failed": <int>, "bestValue": <float|null>, "bestParams": {...}, "space": [...as plan], "importance": {"evaluator": "<name>", "seed": <int|null>, "values": {"<param>": <float>}}, "importanceCheck": {"evaluator": "<name>", "values": {...}} | null, "contourParams": ["<param>", ...], "figures": {"contour": "contour.png", "importance": "importance.png", "history": "history.png"}, "optunaVersion": "<x.y.z>", "generatedAt": "<ISO-8601>"}`. Figure paths are relative to the summary's folder; a figure that could not be drawn (fewer than two complete trials, one parameter) is `null` with a reason in `"notes": [...]`.
- **Estimate**: `plan --timing <timing.json>` reads timing schema 1 and reports `trials × arms × outerFolds × maxEpochs × secondsPerUnit` (per host, mean of that host's measurements) as an upper bound ±20%, stating that pruning and early stopping only lower it; a host with no measurement reads `unmeasured`.
